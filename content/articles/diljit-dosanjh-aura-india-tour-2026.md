He announced it from the stage at Wembley. Of course he did. Diljit Dosanjh, the first Punjabi artist to headline London's Wembley Stadium, used that night in September to tell the crowd where he was going next: the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, on 21 November.

That one date has now become a full India leg of the Aura World Tour.

## Every date and venue

- **1 November** · Delhi-NCR · Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium
- **15 November** · Indore · C21 Estate
- **21 November** · Ahmedabad · Narendra Modi Stadium
- **27 November** · Mumbai · Mahalaxmi Race Course
- **29 November** · Bengaluru · NICE Grounds
- **5 December** · Lucknow · 19th National Jamboree Grounds
- **Chandigarh** · seventh show, date to be announced

## How the tickets work

Tickets are sold on BookMyShow, which is co-producing and promoting the India leg with Live Nation. A RuPay credit card presale runs first. For Ahmedabad, listings started at ₹999.

Business Today puts the Narendra Modi Stadium's capacity at around 1.32 lakh. That makes Ahmedabad the night to watch: no Indian artist books the world's biggest cricket ground to play to empty tiers.

## What he'll play

ThePrint lists G.O.A.T., Ikk Kudi, Lalkara, Lover, 5 Taara, Case and Water among the songs expected on the setlist.

## Why it matters

The Aura tour started in April in Vancouver and has since run through North America, the UK, Europe, Australia and New Zealand, including Madison Square Garden. His last India run, Dil-Luminati in 2024, was a sell-out story and a ticket-scam story at the same time. Expect demand on the same scale, so buy only on the official platform.
