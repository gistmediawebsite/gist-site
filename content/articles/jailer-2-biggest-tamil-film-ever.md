Rajinikanth already owned the record for the biggest Tamil film ever. Now he has taken it from himself.

*Jailer 2*, Nelson Dilipkumar's sequel to the 2023 hit, crossed ₹710 crore worldwide on its 11th day, 25 October, overtaking Shankar's *2.0* (2018) and its reported ₹691 crore. Sacnilk's figures, carried by The Hollywood Reporter India, The Week and others, also make it the first Tamil film past ₹700 crore.

## The run so far

Figures from trade tracker Sacnilk, as reported:

- **Opening day (15 October):** ₹59.7 crore India net, the film's biggest day
- **Global opening weekend:** about $31.2 million, second only to Hollywood's *Street Fighter* worldwide, per ARY News citing Rentrak estimates
- **After 11 days:** ₹466.85 crore India gross, ₹243.50 crore overseas, ₹710.35 crore worldwide
- **After 14 days:** about ₹492 crore India gross and ₹420 crore net, per The Week

Sacnilk's tracker now shows about ₹790 crore worldwide. Overseas, the Middle East has crossed $8 million and North America about $7 million, as per Sacnilk.

## The asterisk on the record

*2.0*'s producers never officially confirmed its lifetime gross. The ₹691 crore figure is Sacnilk's; some reports put *2.0* closer to ₹800 crore. Trade analyst Ramesh Bala told The CSR Journal that older figures may be rounded estimates. So the crown is real on tracked numbers, and debatable on folklore. With *Jailer 2* heading for ₹800 crore anyway, the debate may settle itself.

## The market that sat it out

Here is the twist. PVR INOX and Cinepolis did not screen the Hindi version outside South India, because the producers reportedly agreed a four-week gap before streaming. The national chains insist on eight weeks for Hindi releases, The Week reported, and fear one exception would break the rule.

The result, per Sacnilk: ₹39.95 crore Hindi net in 12 days. *2.0* made ₹188.23 crore in Hindi over its lifetime. Producer and analyst G Dhananjayan has argued about ₹200 crore was left on the table in North India, enough, he says, to push the film past ₹1,000 crore. Sacnilk's own analysis is more sceptical: even with full multiplex access, Hindi audiences per show were far below what *Pushpa 2* drew.

Even so, theatres in the North dropped Hindi releases *Nayyi Navelli* and *Prahaar* to add *Jailer 2* shows, according to Sacnilk.

## The big screen, and the small one

Sun Pictures says Rajinikanth joined fans at an IMAX show of the film, his first time watching with an audience in a theatre in 40 years, as reported by The Hollywood Reporter India.

The streaming home is reportedly Amazon Prime Video, around mid-November, per Esquire India; the makers have not confirmed it. Before that, the film faces *Ramayana: Part 1*, which takes over premium screens from 5 November.

## Why it matters

A Tamil film has posted India's second-biggest worldwide gross of 2026, behind only *Dhurandhar: The Revenge*, per The Week's 26 October count, while skipping the country's biggest multiplex chains in Hindi. That is a win for regional cinema and a warning for the windows fight. Producers want streaming money sooner. Theatres want time. *Jailer 2* has shown both sides what the bill looks like.

*Figures are trade estimates and may be revised.*
