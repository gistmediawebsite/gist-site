Ten years ago an international act in India was an event. This winter it's a scheduling problem. Here's everything announced so far, in date order, so you can plan leave, flights and friendships accordingly.

## November

- **1 Nov · Delhi-NCR:** Diljit Dosanjh opens his Aura India tour at Jawaharlal Nehru Stadium.
- **14 Nov · Bengaluru:** Guns N' Roses.
- **15 Nov · Indore:** Diljit Dosanjh.
- **17 Nov · Guwahati:** Guns N' Roses. Yes, Guwahati.
- **21 Nov · Ahmedabad:** Diljit Dosanjh at the Narendra Modi Stadium.
- **21 Nov · Mumbai:** Anyma brings ÆDEN to Mahalaxmi Racecourse, the same night as Diljit in Ahmedabad.
- **27 Nov · Mumbai** and **29 Nov · Bengaluru:** Diljit Dosanjh.

## December

- **5 Dec · Delhi-NCR:** Fred again.. makes his India debut at Leisure Valley Grounds. Diljit plays Lucknow the same night.
- **9 Dec · Mumbai:** Fred again.. at Mahalaxmi Race Course.
- **13 Dec · Bengaluru:** Fred again.. closes at NICE Grounds, while **Khalid** makes his long-delayed India debut at HUDA Gymkhana Club, Gurugram. His 2020 debut was cancelled by the pandemic.
- **15 Dec · Bengaluru:** Khalid.
- **17 Dec · Mumbai:** Khalid at NESCO.
- **18 Dec · Mumbai:** The Chainsmokers headline Sunburn at Mahalaxmi Racecourse.
- **19 Dec · Delhi:** The Chainsmokers at the Indian Sneaker Festival.
- **20 Dec · Bengaluru:** The Chainsmokers.

## January

- **29 Jan · Bengaluru** and **31 Jan · Mumbai:** Foo Fighters' India debut, the second at Mahalaxmi Racecourse.

## The clashes to plan around

**21 November** forces a choice between Diljit in a 1.3-lakh-seat cricket stadium and Anyma's visual-heavy techno spectacle in Mumbai. **13 December** splits Bengaluru and Gurugram between Fred again.. and Khalid.
