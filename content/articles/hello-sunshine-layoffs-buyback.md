Hello Sunshine, the media company founded by actress Reese Witherspoon, has laid off about 50% of its employees, as per reports. The cuts come after Witherspoon and the European studio group Mediawan bought the company back. The number of affected staff is reported to be roughly 30.

## What has been reported

As per reports, the layoffs followed the buyback of the company from Candle Media. One report cites a source for the staff cuts. Neither Hello Sunshine nor Mediawan has commented publicly in the reports so far, so which teams are affected remains unclear.

## The money trail

Candle Media, which is backed by Blackstone and run by former Disney executives Kevin Mayer and Tom Staggs, valued Hello Sunshine at $900 million when it bought the company in 2021.

The buyback price has been reported as "low to mid eight figures". Using $50 million as an estimate, one report calculates that this would be a decline of roughly 94% from the original valuation. That $50 million figure is an estimate used in that report, not a disclosed price.

Put simply, a company valued at $900 million in 2021 has reportedly returned to its founder at a small fraction of that number, and half the team has gone with it.

## Who bears the cost

The valuation hit sits with the owners who bought in. The job losses sit with about 30 employees who did not set the price in either deal. Companies often cut staff after ownership changes, but the reports do not say whether further restructuring is planned.

## What happens next

The reports do not detail Hello Sunshine's plans under the new ownership. Whether the leaner company changes its slate of film and television projects is not yet known. Official confirmation of the final price and the scope of the cuts would settle the open questions.

## Why it matters

The 2021 deal was pitched as proof that a founder-led, celebrity-fronted studio could command a premium valuation. The reported buyback price shows how far that premium has shrunk.
